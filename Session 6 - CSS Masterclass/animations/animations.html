Animation settings

The box rests as purple, but the keyframes start gold and end tomato. Add a delay with backwards to see gold early, or forwards to keep the end state.

scroll() ties progress to how far the box on the right is scrolled; view() ties it to the element moving through the visible area. Time-based duration and delay don't apply in those modes.

Changing a setting restarts the animation (except play-state).
